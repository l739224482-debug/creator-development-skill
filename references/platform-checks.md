# Platform Checks

## YouTube

Use the canonical channel ID or resolved channel URL, not a search-result ID. Public surfaces may support channel identity, subscriber/video counts, recent uploads, descriptions, disclosed links, and content review. Audience geography, private analytics, rates, and conversion remain unknown unless an authorized source exposes them.

## TikTok

Use the resolved public profile and platform identity; handles can change. Public surfaces may support visible followers, posts, captions, recent activity, bio links, and content review. Audience composition, exact commerce performance, and private contact data remain unknown without authorized evidence.

## Instagram

Use the resolved public profile and platform identity. Public surfaces may support bio, visible posts/reels, captions, activity, and disclosed contact buttons or links. Hidden insights, audience geography, story analytics, and private emails remain unknown.

Access failure is not negative evidence. Mark the affected field `unknown` or `pending_review`, record the observation date, and do not promote the record past a gating requirement.
